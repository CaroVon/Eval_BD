# Findings so far (2026-10-30)

Synthesis of RESULTS.md sections 1-26 for deciding the paper's claims. Numbers are from
fp32 batch-invariant vLLM on A100 unless stated. "Holds" means supported by data on three parent
families (Qwen2.5-3B, Phi-3.5-mini, SmolLM2-1.7B) and, for Qwen, two tasks.

## What holds

1. **Exact screening is exact.** Lemma 3 had zero violations in every truth matrix (Qwen GSM8K
   1,319 and MMLU-Pro 1,000, Phi GSM8K 1,319; four ladder versions each): no flips outside D, and
   continuation from t\* reproduces the child output exactly. Ties are broken by smallest token id,
   as definition 2 requires.
2. **Determinism needs checking per model family and mode.** Batch-invariant mode passes all
   four layers for Qwen in compiled mode, but Phi-3.5 varies between processes in compiled mode
   (5/200 questions) and passes only with `--enforce-eager`. Without batch invariance, 4.5% of
   Qwen answers flip from a batch-size change alone. bf16 logits create frequent exact ties that
   inflate D; fp32 removes them (Q8_0 |A|/|P| 0.717 -> 0.478 on the pilot).
3. **A quantitative law for screening power.** Divergence happens where the parent is nearly
   indifferent, and quantization acts as additive noise of sd sigma_q on the margin, nearly the same
   across families for a given bit width (Q8 0.07-0.08, Q6 0.23-0.26, Q4 0.83-0.90 logits).
   Per-token divergence rate rho = f_m(0) sigma_q / sqrt(2 pi) (verified on all four parent x task
   pairs: Qwen GSM8K and MMLU-Pro, Phi GSM8K, SmolLM2 GSM8K, within about 15%), so
   |A|/|P| ~ 1 - E[exp(-rho T)]. Screening power is set by the parent's own near-tie density f_m(0)
   (Phi 0.07, Qwen 0.12 GSM8K / 0.19 MMLU-Pro, SmolLM2 0.124), the bit width, and output length.
   sigma_q is not predicted by median weight error (SmolLM2 Q4_K_M: 2.5x the error, same sigma).
   50 calibration questions plus parent margins predict |A|/|P| (design information only).
4. **Divergence is mostly harmless wording.** 60-80% of first divergences are word choices,
   10-20% punctuation; ~75% of outputs re-align with the parent within 30 tokens and 72-89% keep
   the same answer. Flips that do occur come from reasoning paths that drift after an early
   wording change, not from answer-token near-ties: the parent is confident at the answer
   position for almost every flipped question.
5. **Equal-compute advantage exists in a narrow regime.** For Q8_0 on GSM8K (all three families), at a
   per-version budget of 0.6-0.8 of full evaluation, the screened harm certificate reaches the exact
   H (Qwen width 0.003 vs 0.024 for B1) and the screened net certificate reaches the exact theta.
   The break-even audit rate is pi* = 0.59 (Qwen, r = 0.365). Below that, B1 is cheaper.

## What does not hold as planned

6. **E2/E3: structural gain is small outside Q8_0.** Q6_K |A|/|P| 0.78-0.98, Q4_K_M and below
   ~1.0; continuation saves 0.5-5% for low bits. Prefill along the parent output costs about half
   a full generation in batched offline evaluation (r = 0.365 A100, 0.29 H100), because batched
   decode is already efficient. Exact screening must touch every parent-correct question, so B1
   wins at the audit rates of coarse slices (pi about 0.33).
7. **Zero-harm design certifies nothing at tau = 0.05.** Every version has real flips; worst-slice
   bounds stay at 0.10 (GSM8K) / 0.2 (MMLU-Pro) for Q8_0 under the cover design.
8. **Harm-only certificates are structurally limited.** When repairs are common (Qwen flips are
   near-symmetric), any certificate valid for H cannot be tau-precise for theta once Rep >= tau.
   The net certificate (docs/net_certificate.md) fixes validity and certifies GSM8K Q6_K and
   MMLU-Pro Q8_0/Q6_K, but only near full-evaluation cost. Flip symmetry itself is model-dependent
   (Qwen about even, Phi mostly harms, SmolLM2 Q8_0 mostly repairs). On MMLU-Pro 3,000 with 23 slices
   the net certificate passes Q8_0 in 37% of reps at a 0.95 floor; the harm certificate never does.
9. **Cascade drafts and re-anchored verification do not rescue low bits.** Quants are independent
   perturbations of the parent (adjacent quants are not closer). Re-anchored exact verification gains
   about 1.5x for Q8_0 with measured short-chunk costs, nothing below Q6_K.
10. **MMLU-Pro is not "medium length"** for Qwen2.5-3B CoT (mean 466 tokens, 7.2% truncated at 1,024
    by long calculations), which hurts screening.

## Implications for the claims (for discussion)

- The theory (certificate validity, lower bounds, structure-prediction separation) is unaffected.
- The practical claim should be narrowed to: high bit-width versions, fine-grained simultaneous
  certification (small slices or tau <= 0.02), and budgets near full evaluation, where exact screening
  gives several-fold tighter worst-slice bounds at equal compute.
- New contributions supported by data: the margin-noise law for screening power (with a cheap
  predictor), the determinism findings (bf16 ties, per-family compile mode), and the net-degradation
  certificate with the harm-certificate impossibility.
- G3 as stated (1.5x over the strongest baseline) is met only inside the regime above.
